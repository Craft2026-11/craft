01 / THE ENGINEERING LOOP

Diagnose across stages.
Verify in the tools.

From synthesis to routing, CRAFT connects observations to constrained actions, then brings real EDA feedback into the next decision.

01 / ENVIRONMENTExecute the design flow

RTL, PDK and timing constraints enter synthesis through routing. Reports and compatible checkpoints carry evidence forward.

02 / OPTIMIZATIONTurn evidence into actions

Diagnose bottlenecks, commit predictions, execute constrained interventions and independently evaluate their effects.

03 / ADAPTATIONKeep experience accountable

Trace decisions and validate bounded skill revisions before promotion. The underlying model weights remain unchanged.

The manuscript describes a bounded skill revision for commercial 28 nm CPU Tile, motivated by three unsuccessful executions.

  1. 3 unsuccessful executionsMotivate a revision of evidence and action priorities.
  2. 5 paired shadow casesCompare incumbent and revised decisions on identical inputs.
  3. 3 behavioral changesIncluding two action-selection changes; the revision meets the validation criteria and is promoted.

Shadow decisions are not executed on EDA tools. This study evaluates decision behavior; a causal PPA benefit requires separate executed comparisons. It is distinct from the Figure 4 example above.
